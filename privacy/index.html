Privacy policy

Effective: 16 November 2026 · Magyar változat

This policy explains what data the Familia mobile app (the app) handles, why, for how long, and what your rights are. The app is currently in a closed beta. When new features need new kinds of data, we will update this policy and its date.

Data controller

Kornél Kovács, private individual
Email: hello@hellofamilia.eu

What data we handle

DataWhy
Email address (or Apple / Google account identifier)Signing in, identifying your account, sending the sign-in code.
Family members' names, colours and roles; the family's nameSo family members can see each other and know who a task or event is for.
Date of birth (if you add it)Showing the birthday and sending a reminder.
Shopping lists, items and who buys themRunning the shared list.
Calendar events (title, time, participants, driver, note) and special days (birthdays, name days, anniversaries)Running the family calendar and reminders.
The device token needed for notifications (push token) and your chosen languageSending notifications (e.g. "I'm at the shop", reminders).
Time of "I'm at the shop" alerts, invite codesPreventing abuse (e.g. too many notifications, guessing codes) and joining a family.

Profiles of children without a phone are created and managed by a parent; children do not sign in. The app shows no ads, does no tracking and collects no location data. We do not sell data or use it for marketing.

Only members of your own family can see your family's data. Within a family, signed-in adults can see and change the family's shared data. Your family members can also see the email address you signed in with; if you sign in with Apple using a hidden address, they only see that you use an Apple account.

Legal basis

We process data to provide the service you request by signing up (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Data about children and other family members (e.g. name, birthday) is entered by the user who adds them, who should only do so if entitled to (e.g. as a parent).

Some providers may process data outside the European Union (in the United States); such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or adequacy decision.

How long we keep data

As long as your account exists. You can delete your account in the app at any time (Settings → Delete account); your account and its data are deleted immediately. If no other signed-in member remains in the family, all of the family's data is deleted too. Data disappears from the hosting provider's backups within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for information about the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, restrict its processing, or give it to you in a machine-readable format; you can also object to processing. Write to hello@hellofamilia.eu; we reply within 30 days.

If you think the processing is unlawful, you can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, naih.hu) or to the supervisory authority where you live, or go to court.
