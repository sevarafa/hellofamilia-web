Delete your Familia account

Familia – Family Organizer · developer: Familia (Kornél Kovács) · Magyar változat

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Familia and sign in.
  2. Tap the gear icon in the top right corner (Settings).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete permanently.

Delete by email (if you no longer have the app)

Write to hello@hellofamilia.eu from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete account". If you signed in with Apple or Google, write from that account's email address. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address, Apple or Google ID).
  • Your family member profile (name, color, date of birth).
  • The device IDs stored for notifications, your invites and your join requests.
  • If no other signed-in member remains in the family: all of the family's data, including lists, calendar, special days and children's profiles.

What is kept

  • If another signed-in member remains in the family, the shared lists, events and special days stay with them, as they belong to the whole family. They are no longer linked to your name.
  • Data disappears from the hosting provider's backups within 30 days.
